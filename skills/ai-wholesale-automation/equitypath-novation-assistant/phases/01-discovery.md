# Phase 1 — DISCOVERY CALL

> **Goal:** Gather property info, uncover motivation & pain points, find the Magic Problem, get asking price, and hand off to "underwriting." NO negotiation on this call.

> **Mindset:** Treat every lead like it's the only one you have. Be positive, don't prejudge, set yourself apart, and ALIGN with the seller.

---

## STEP 1 — INTRODUCTION

**Say:**
> "Hi [Seller Name], this is Carlos, how are you doing today?"

> "Excellent."

**If warm handoff from JV partner:**
> "Nice to meet you, Mr./Mrs. [Name]. My name is Carlos and I'll be taking care of you today. [JV Partner] tapped me on the shoulder because the investment group I work with has a different business model than a lot of other investors, and sometimes we can make deals work that are not a fit for them."

> "My job is real simple — I just need to gather some basic information about the property to see if we might be a fit to make an offer on it."

`[COACHING: Be warm, upbeat. First impression sets the tone for everything.]`

---

## STEP 2 — SET THE STAGE

**Say:**
> "Before we get started, real quick — a little about us. We are a creative Real Estate Investment company that specializes in speed and convenience, and we pride ourselves in creating win-win scenarios. We live by the golden rule. So if we end up being a match to buy the property, we can make everything very easy and straightforward. And if for whatever reason the property doesn't fit our buying criteria, I will still do everything in my power to help you out and try to point you in the right direction. Does that sound fair?"

> "Perfect."

`[COACHING: This sets YOU apart from every other investor who just calls to lowball. You're positioned as a problem-solver, not a vulture.]`

---

## STEP 3 — OCCUPANCY

**Ask:**
> "Are you living in the property currently?"

**If YES → Ask:**
> "Did you have an idea — if you could wave a magic wand and everything went perfect — how quickly would you like to sell the property, move out, and have everything complete and the money in your bank account?"

**If NO → Ask:**
> "Is anyone living in the house or is it vacant right now?"

**If TENANT → Ask:**
> "Are they on a month-to-month or annual lease right now?"
> "If we were to keep them as tenants, are they current on their rent and how much is that right now?"
> "If you could wave a magic wand and everything went perfect, how quickly would you like to sell the property and have everything complete and the money in your bank account?"

`[COACHING: The "magic wand" question reveals their TRUE timeline. Listen for urgency.]`
`[🔥 FLAG any urgency — moving, financial pressure, life event, inherited property]`

---

## STEP 4 — PROPERTY CONDITION & REPAIRS

**Say:**
> "Our process is very simple. I'm going to ask some questions about the house — if you don't know the answer, no problem, just answer as best you can. Sound good?"

**Run through each item. For anything needing repair, ASK:**
> "Have you had a chance to see how much that would cost to update/fix?"

`[COACHING: This gets them "talking money" and thinking about what a traditional sale would cost them. Every repair = leverage for your offer later.]`

### Property Checklist:
- [ ] **Beds/Baths:** "How many bedrooms and bathrooms?"
- [ ] **Sq Ft:** "It says the property is ___ square feet on the tax records, does that sound right?"
- [ ] **Roof:** "Can you tell me about the age and condition of the roof? Last time it was inspected?"
- [ ] **HVAC:** "Do you know the age of the heating and cooling system? Any issues or repairs?"
- [ ] **Plumbing/Electrical:** "What's the age of the plumbing and electrical? Any recent inspections or repairs?"
- [ ] **Kitchen:** "When was the kitchen last remodeled?"
  - Cabinets? Countertops? Backsplash? Flooring? Island? Appliances (white/black/stainless/mixed)?
- [ ] **Bathrooms:** "Any updates that may affect the value?"
  - Shower/tub? Vanity? Flooring?
- [ ] **Flooring:** "Walk me through the type of flooring throughout the house — approximate age and condition?"
- [ ] **Exterior:** "What type of exterior does the house have? Age and condition?"
- [ ] **Windows:** "Do you know the age of the windows?"
- [ ] **Landscaping:** "Can you tell me a bit about the landscaping outside?"
- [ ] **Other:** "Are there any other repairs, maintenance, or anything else you'd like to disclose that would affect the value — just so everything is out in the open from the very beginning?"

`[COACHING: Take detailed notes here. This feeds directly into your comp analysis for Call 2 and your repair estimate for the deal calc.]`

---

## STEP 5 — MOTIVATION (THE MAGIC PROBLEM)

**Say:**
> "I appreciate you giving me that information, thanks! If you don't mind me asking — what would an ideal situation look like for you with the sale of your property?"

`[COACHING: SHUT UP AND LISTEN. Let them talk as long as possible. Do NOT interrupt.]`
`[🔥 Listen for: financial stress, divorce, inheritance, relocation, health issues, tired landlord, behind on payments, code violations, family conflict]`
`[Note: personality type, timeline pressure, who else is involved in the decision]`

**After they finish, reflect back:**
> "Ok, I see. If I'm hearing you right — [summarize their situation with empathy, restate their pain points and their goals once they sell the property]?"

`[COACHING: This is the most important part of Call 1. The Magic Problem is what you'll use in Call 2 and Call 3 to drive urgency and close the deal. Get it RIGHT.]`

### "Scorch the Earth" Qualifier:
**Ask:**
> "What happens if you don't sell in the next 60 days?"

`[🔥 This is Rich Wonders' key disqualification technique. It exposes whether the seller is truly motivated or just shopping around.]`
`[No urgency in their answer = likely not a real deal. Consider disqualifying and moving on.]`
`[Strong urgency (foreclosure, relocation deadline, divorce timeline, financial pressure) = GREEN LIGHT — this is a real novation candidate.]`

---

## STEP 6 — ASKING PRICE

**Say:**
> "Ok great, no problem, thank you. Just so I know — if we were able to make a cash offer for this property, if we would pay all commissions and closing costs, and if we would be able to close on a timeline that works for you... if you didn't have to lift a finger or spend one penny on repairs, fees, or commissions — did you have an idea on what your bottom dollar would be for an ideal situation offer like that?"

**After they answer (slight pause):**
> "Ok... thank you. And just so I know... how did you come to that number for your asking price?"

`[💰 Capture their number AND their reasoning. This tells you if they're emotional or data-driven.]`

**Then defuse the price conversation:**
> "No problem. Just so you know, I'm just a glorified 'information gatherer.' We have a separate department that determines what we could pay for the property. They'll decide if we can make an offer, and for how much. They determine our offer independent of this conversation — they just like me to ask in case you wanted 10 million dollars for the property, so we'd know we weren't a fit and I wouldn't put them to work on it."

*[smile and pause]*

> "They use the 'Fannie Mae' appraisal guidelines and do a comparative market analysis of the property using recent market data in the immediate area to see what we'd be able to pay and what investment strategy would be the best fit. Does that make sense?"

`[COACHING: The "underwriting team" frame is CRITICAL. It creates authority, removes you from being the bad guy, and sets up the comp discussion in Call 2.]`

---

## STEP 7 — SETUP FOR CALLBACK

**If seller is ready:**
> "Ok great. I'm going to get started right away. Let me pass your file over to my underwriting team and let them start working on it. I can normally get a final response within an hour or so, and let you know if we're able to make an offer, and how much, and what options might be available to you."

**If seller is NOT ready:**
Summarize the reason they said they weren't ready, confirm you heard correctly, then say:
> "Perfectly ok, thank you for sharing that with me. No worries at all."

**Pre-close / commitment test:**
> "Ok, let me do this... I'm going to pass your file forward to the underwriting team. They're the ones that run all the numbers and make the final decision. Before I put them to work, I just want to make sure — if I'm able to get to a price and terms that work for you and work for us, is there ANYTHING stopping you from moving forward with paperwork today?"

`[⚠️ If yes — find out why and discuss. This reveals hidden objections early.]`

**Set timeline:**
> "Ok. I'm going to get started right away. We've had a large influx of properties being submitted because of the recent market shift, but typically we can get a final decision within [timeframe], but if they're backed up, I should be able to get back to you in 24 hours at the latest."

> "If the property is approved, I'll be able to send over a formal written offer via email and walk you through it. If it's not approved for whatever reason, I'll let you know why and still try to help however I can and point you in the right direction. Does that sound good?"

**Close:**
> "Perfect. I'm going to text you with my name and you can save my contact information in your phone so you'll know it's me when I call you back, and won't think it's a telemarketer. Pleasure speaking to you and we'll get back to you as soon as we have the final word."

`[COACHING: Send the intro text immediately after hanging up.]`
