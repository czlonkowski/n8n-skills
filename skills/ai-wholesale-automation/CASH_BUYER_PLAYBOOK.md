## Method 1: PropStream Filter Setup

```
PropStream > Search Filter > Quick List: Cash Buyers
Then stack:
  Last Sale Date: Last 12-24 months (active buyers only)
  Properties Owned: 5-15 (serious investors, not one-timers)
  Property Type: Residential > SFR
  Classification: LLC / Corporate (skip individuals for speed)
  Geography: Zip codes in your target area

Export > Skip trace ($0.12-$0.15/record) > Import to Podio
```

Start with 5-10 core zip codes per market. Filter hard — 50 active verified buyers beats 500 stale contacts.

## Method 2: Title Company Referrals

Call your escrow officer and say:

> "Who are your top 3 cash investors closing deals this month? I want to bring them wholesale deals — would you be willing to introduce us?"

Title companies are legally allowed to share investor names (public record at closing). They are motivated to help because more deals = more closings for them. This consistently produces the **highest-quality buyers** because you get referrals to people who have literally wired funds and closed in your exact market.

## Proof of Funds Verification

**What a real POF looks like**: Bank or hard-money lender letterhead, dated within 30 days, shows buyer/entity name, available balance >= purchase price + closing costs, institution contact info.

**Verification steps**:
1. Request POF before adding buyer to VIP list
2. Call phone number on letterhead to verify
3. Check entity name matches assignment contract
4. For hard-money POF: confirm lender funds in your market

**Red flags** (cut from list immediately):
- POF dated more than 60 days ago
- Screenshot of Coinbase or crypto balance
- Generic template with no bank letterhead
- Can't close in claimed timeframe

## VIP Tier System

Segment Podio buyer list into 3 tiers:

| Tier | Criteria | Access | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VIP (Tier 1)** | Verified POF + bought 1+ deal in last 6 months | 24-hour first look | 10-20 buyers per market |
| **Active (Tier 2)** | Provided buy box + responded to last 3 blasts | Get deals after VIP 24hr window | 30-50 per market |
| **Cold (Tier 3)** | Never responded or no offer | Blasts only, purge quarterly | Unlimited |

**Goal**: 10 Tier 1 buyers per market (30 total across Phoenix/Houston/LA). With 10 real buyers per market who close 1-3 deals/month, you have a permanent dispo solution for every deal under contract.
