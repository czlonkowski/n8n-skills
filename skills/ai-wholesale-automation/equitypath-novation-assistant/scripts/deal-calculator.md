## NOVATION MAO FORMULA (Rich Wonders / Novation King Method)

> **IMPORTANT:** Novation uses AS-IS RETAIL VALUE, not ARV after repairs.
> "As-is retail" = what a retail buyer would pay in CURRENT condition.

### The Formula:
```
Novation MAO = As-Is Retail Value × 90% − Your Novation Fee

The 10% haircut covers:
  ~6%  Agent commissions (listing + buyer agent)
  ~2%  Closing costs
  ~2%  Buyer concessions/credits
```

### Quick Calc:
```
AS-IS RETAIL VALUE:                $________
× 0.90 =                          $________ (max seller can net after costs)
− YOUR FEE ($25K-$45K target):    -$________
                                   ─────────
= SELLER NET OFFER:                $________

YOUR NOVATION FEE:                 $________
TARGET: $25,000 - $30,000+ minimum
```

### Example A — Phoenix AZ, $350K as-is retail:
```
As-Is Retail:           $350,000
× 90% =                $315,000  (seller's max net after all costs)
Your Fee:              -$30,000
                        ─────────
SELLER NET OFFER:       $285,000
YOU EARN:               $30,000 ✅

Present as: "You get $285K net in 60 days vs. $240K cash today"
```

## DECISION MATRIX: CASH vs. NOVATION

| Factor | Cash/Wholesale | Novation |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Seller wants low price | ✅ Good fit | ❌ Not needed |
| Seller wants top dollar | ❌ Won't work | ✅ Perfect fit |
| Property needs heavy repairs | ✅ Cash buyer wants discount | ❌ Harder to sell retail |
| Property is retail-ready / cosmetic only | ⚠️ Leaving money on table | ✅ List on MLS at full value |
| Seller needs speed (< 2 weeks) | ✅ Fast close | ⚠️ Novation takes 30-60 days |
| Seller has no mortgage | ✅ Either works | ✅ Either works |
| Seller has high mortgage | ⚠️ Less spread | ⚠️ Less spread |
| Seller declined your cash offer | — | ✅ NOVATION PIVOT |
| PPC / high-intent inbound lead | ✅ Try cash first | ✅ Best novation candidates |
| Cold call / dead lead reactivation | ✅ Try cash first | ✅ Reactivate with novation pitch |

### When to Novate:
- Seller wants more than cash offer allows
- Property is in decent/good condition (retail-ready or close to it)
- As-is retail value supports a $25K+ fee after seller net + costs
- Seller is motivated but price-sensitive
- Lead came from PPC (highest-intent, best novation conversion)
- Dead wholesale lead who said "your cash offer is too low"

### When to Cash/Assign:
- Property needs significant work (not retail-ready)
- Seller accepts low offer
- Speed is the priority (< 2 weeks)
- As-is retail doesn't support novation spread

## NOVATION TIMELINE

```
Day 0:    Seller signs PSA + Novation Agreement + POA + Marketing Agreement
Day 1-2:  Get photos & measurements from seller
Day 3-5:  Hire listing agent, prep MLS listing
Day 5-7:  Listed on MLS
Day 7-21: Showings, receive offers, vet buyers
Day 21-30: Accept offer, open escrow, buyer inspection/due diligence
Day 30-60: Close → Seller gets net, you get fee from escrow (HUD-1)
```
