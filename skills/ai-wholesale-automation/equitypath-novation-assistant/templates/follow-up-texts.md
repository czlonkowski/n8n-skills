# Follow-Up Text Templates

---

## After Call 1 — Discovery

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos with Equity Path Offers. Great speaking with you today about [Property Address]. I've passed your file to our underwriting team and I'll be back in touch as soon as I have an update. Feel free to call or text anytime. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

---

## After Call 2 — Anchor

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos. Thanks for going over everything with me again today. I'm working with the team right now to see what we can put together for you. I'll be in touch very shortly. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

---

## After Call 3 — Signed / Under Contract

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos with Equity Path Offers. Congratulations and thank you for trusting us with your property at [Address]! As discussed, if you can get those photos over in the next 48 hours, we'll get everything moving right away. I'm your point of contact throughout — don't hesitate to reach out. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

---

## After Call 3 — Seller Needs Time

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos. I completely understand wanting to take some time to think it over. I sent everything to your email so you can review at your own pace. I'm here whenever you're ready — no pressure at all. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

## Photo Request Reminder (48hr follow-up)

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos! Just checking in — were you able to get those photos of the property? Once we have those, we can get the marketing process started right away. Let me know if you need the instruction video again or if you'd like me to send someone out. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

## General Check-In (Deal in Progress)

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos with Equity Path Offers. Just wanted to give you a quick update on your property at [Address]. [Insert update — marketing started, showings scheduled, offer received, etc.]. Everything is moving along. Let me know if you have any questions! 📞 [State Phone Number]
```

## Warm Follow-Up (Cold Lead Re-Engage)

```
Hey [Seller Name], it's Carlos with Equity Path Offers. We spoke a while back about your property on [Address]. I wanted to check in and see if your situation has changed at all. If you're still considering selling, I'd love to see if we can make something work. No pressure — just here to help. 📞 [State Phone Number]
```
