# Phase 3 — DELIVER (The Novation Close)

> **Goal:** Present the low cash offer as a takeaway, pivot to the "Concierge Service" novation pitch as the exciting upgrade, walk through 4 documents, get signatures, and set post-close expectations.

> **Mindset:** The novation is NOT the backup plan — it's the UPGRADE. You're bringing good news. The cash offer was the anchor; the novation is the reward. Deliver with excitement and confidence.

---

## STEP 1 — RE-INTRODUCTION

**Say:**
> "Hi [Seller Name], this is Carlos with Equity Path Offers, how are you?"

> "That's great."

`[COACHING: Keep it brief. They're expecting the call — get to the point with warmth.]`

---

## STEP 2 — RE-SUMMARIZE SITUATION

**Say:**
> "The reason you're selling the property, you had said [restate their situation and motivation]."

*(Seller confirms)*

> "And you were looking to have everything taken care of [restate their timeline]."

`[COACHING: This is the third time you're reflecting their pain back. It keeps the emotional urgency front and center right before your pitch.]`

---

## STEP 3 — CASH OFFER TAKEAWAY

**Say:**
> "I really want to try to make this work. The first option I submitted the property for was a cash offer, where we would be the final buyer. I've been speaking with the decision makers, and there was a lot of back and forth. We have quite a few properties we're considering right now, and after taking a look at all the details and the data..."

*(pause)*

> "...to be honest, it looks like a cash offer from us is not likely going to be the best fit for a win-win situation. I was able to get a cash offer approval, but it really is quite a bit lower than where you were hoping to be — they would need to be at $[LOW CASH NUMBER] — and I know that's probably not going to work for you."

`[💰 This number should be LOW — it's the anchor that makes the novation number feel amazing by comparison.]`
`[COACHING: Deliver this with slight reluctance and empathy, like you fought for them but the numbers just didn't work for a cash deal.]`

**Then immediately pivot:**

> "HOWEVER... I DO have some good news."

`[COACHING: Pause here. Let the "good news" land. This is the emotional swing from disappointment to hope.]`

---

## STEP 4 — THE NOVATION "CONCIERGE SERVICE" PITCH

**Say:**
> "I was telling you, we're a creative real estate investment company and we have a few different options available. The program I was REALLY hoping I could get the property approved for is called the 'Concierge Service.'"

> "I didn't mention it earlier because they're very selective on what properties are approved — it's very labor and resource intensive for us, and only a small percentage of properties that come across our desk are approved for it."

> "So I was very excited when I was able to get approval for your property!"

`[COACHING: This creates exclusivity and perceived value. They didn't just get an offer — they got ACCEPTED into something special.]`

**Explain the program:**
> "So this is how it works: Essentially, you get ALL the benefits of a cash offer, meaning — you don't have to spend a penny or lift a finger to fix anything. You don't have to pay any commissions, fees, or closing costs."

> "You see, from doing so many transactions in [seller's state], we've developed a robust network of realtors and other investors that we work with. A certain percentage of the properties that come across our desk don't fit our criteria, but they would be a PERFECT MATCH for another buyer in our network."

> "The way it would work is — we would agree to a NET PRICE with you. We would pay all the closing costs, fees, and any commissions involved. We would resell our contract to a third party, and negotiate and earn our fee from the BUYER."

> "It's a 'HANDS OFF,' WHITE GLOVE EXPERIENCE, where we deliver the result ON A SILVER PLATTER."

**The money shot:**
> "So essentially, the experience and net result would be the same as a cash offer, but you'll be PLEASANTLY SURPRISED with how much more you'll be netting."

`[COACHING: This is THE line. "Same experience, more money." That's the entire novation sell in one sentence.]`

---

## STEP 5 — TRANSITION TO DOCUMENTS

**Say:**
> "I have everything written up in BLACK AND WHITE. It's company policy that I send it over with us on the phone so I can make sure it comes through, and we can go over the details together and I can answer any questions you may have."

> "Your email address is [confirm email], right?"

> "OK PERFECT. I will send it through RIGHT NOW."

`[COACHING: Have the documents READY to send before this call. No fumbling.]`

**Clarify the fee structure (transparency builds trust):**
> "Just to be clear — we wouldn't be representing you as a realtor. We would be reselling our contract, which I know most people don't care how we make money, as long as they get every penny they're promised. We would earn our fee from the buyer and handle all the heavy lifting — you can sit back and collect the check."

---

## STEP 6 — WALK THROUGH DOCUMENTS (4-Document Stack)

> **The 4 documents:** PSA → Novation Agreement → POA → Marketing Agreement
> Have ALL four loaded and ready to send BEFORE this call.

### Document 1: Purchase & Sale Agreement (PSA)
**Say:**
> "Ok, the first document I'm sending over is the Purchase Agreement."

> "I just want to explain the major points. The first doc is the 'as-is' purchase and sale agreement."

*(Walk through the purchase agreement line by line with the seller)*

`[COACHING: Go through key terms: property address, net price to seller, as-is condition, closing timeline, who pays what. The PSA must include: "Buyer reserves the right to novate or substitute themselves for a third-party end buyer." Be thorough but not overwhelming.]`

### Document 2: Novation Agreement / Addendum
**Say:**
> "The second agreement is the Novation Agreement. This agreement instructs the title company what to do if we end up selling the property to someone that's buying using a loan. So the third-party buyer can pay with cash, or buy using a loan, but either way it won't affect our deal and you'll get every penny you were promised."

> "The only thing that will come out of your end is any mortgage, taxes, or liens against the property. Is there anything like that on the property that you are aware of?"

`[💰 Capture any outstanding mortgage balance, liens, or tax issues here. This affects your spread calculation.]`
`[COACHING: This is where the seller consents to you substituting an end buyer. They are released from dealing with the new buyer directly — you handle everything.]`

### Document 3: Power of Attorney (POA)
**Say:**
> "The third document is a Power of Attorney. This allows us to list the property on the MLS on your behalf — it's where all the realtors in your market look for properties, and it's a great tool to help us get the deal done as fast as humanly possible."

> "Don't worry — we'll pay for any realtor fees or commission involved. You will get every penny that you're promised."

`[COACHING: POA must be notarized. Some title companies require limits on scope — keep the POA narrow (marketing and listing only, not full authority). If the seller asks "why do you need power of attorney?" → "It simply allows us to handle the listing paperwork so you don't have to deal with any of that. You stay hands-off."]`

### Document 4: Marketing Agreement
**Say:**
> "And the last document is our Marketing Agreement. This simply outlines the marketing rights and how the process works. Just to be clear — we wouldn't be representing you as a realtor. We would be reselling our contract, which I know most people don't care how we make money, as long as they get every penny they're promised. We earn our fee from the buyer and handle all the heavy lifting — you can sit back and collect the check."

`[COACHING: The Marketing Agreement discloses your novation fee to all parties. This is REQUIRED in Texas and recommended everywhere. Transparency builds trust — don't shy away from it.]`

`[⚠️ Also required for retail-style sales: Property Condition Disclosure (seller fills out), Lead Paint Disclosure (pre-1978 homes), HOA docs if applicable. Handle these after signing or send separately.]`

---

## STEP 7 — GET SIGNATURES

**Say:**
> "Ok great. Now if you can please scroll back up to page one, there's a space at the bottom left-hand corner for your initials. You can either do what's called 'adopt and sign' — one of their pre-made generic cursive initials — or if you're feeling adventurous, you can try to draw it in with your finger or mouse. Either way is equally valid."

*(Walk them through if needed)*

> "I need your initials at the bottom of the first four pages, please."

*(Pause for them to do it)*

> "At the bottom of the fourth page is also a place for your world-famous autograph as well."

`[COACHING: Keep it light and conversational during signing. The "world famous autograph" line keeps the mood positive. If they hesitate or have objections, address directly and confidently — don't rush, don't pressure, just handle it.]`

**Common signing objections:**
- "I want to think about it" → "I totally understand. What specifically would you like to think over? I want to make sure I've answered everything."
- "I need to talk to my spouse" → "Of course. Would it be helpful if I jumped on a quick call with both of you so I can answer any questions directly?"
- "This seems too good to be true" → "I appreciate the honesty. Here's exactly how it works and how we make money — [re-explain fee from buyer]. I can also provide references from previous sellers if that would help."

---

## STEP 8 — SET EXPECTATIONS (Post-Signature)

**Say:**
> "Awesome, congratulations! Happy to be working with you. I'll be taking care of you throughout this process. I just wanted to cover a few important things and set the expectations for the rest of the process, is that ok?"

### Communication:
> "First off, I wanted to emphasize the importance of communication in this process. You have my word — if you reach out to me and I'm not able to pick up, you'll never go days and days without hearing back. I'm on the phone quite a bit throughout the day, so I'm likely wrapping up another call, but I'll always make you my next call and get back to you same day, normally within an hour."

> "Also, if I ever reach out to you, it will always be for a specific reason having to do with your file. So if you could please get back to me as soon as you're able to. Communication is very important for everything to go as smoothly as possible. If we can make that commitment to be good about that, it will make life easier for both of us. Does that work for you?"

### Photos & Measurements:
> "Ok great! Another thing — we'll be needing to get pictures of the property. Normally it's quickest if the seller is able to take them. I have a video I can send with the instructions of what we need. We're also going to need to get the measurements of the rooms — it can be done with an ordinary tape measure and should only take a few minutes. If you aren't able to, no worries, I can hire someone to come out and do it if needed."

### Showings & Security:
> "Thirdly, just so you know, we will NEVER EVER send anyone to the house unannounced. Once in a blue moon, someone that sees our marketing will take it upon themselves to show up at the doorstep and ignore our instructions. If that were to happen, you do not need to let them in — just let us know about it right away so we can handle the situation."

### Process Overview:
> "So a quick overview of what we discussed — first step is we get the pictures, then we start our marketing process, next we vet buyers and offers, set up a few walkthroughs, accept an offer, and close. Do you have any questions for me right now?"
