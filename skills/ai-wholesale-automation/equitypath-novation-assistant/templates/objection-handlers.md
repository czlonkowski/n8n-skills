# Objection Handlers — Quick Reference

> Use during live calls. When a seller raises an objection, find it here and pick your response.

---

## PRICE OBJECTIONS

### "That's too low"
**Option A (Empathy + Data):**
> "I completely understand, and I hear you. The thing is, the data in the area is showing [restate comp info]. After you factor in closing costs, commissions, and any repairs, the net-to-seller on a traditional sale would actually be pretty close to what we're talking about here — and you'd have to wait [X] months and deal with all the hassle."

**Option B (Reframe to Net):**
> "I totally get it. Let me ask you this — when you say that number, are you thinking about what you'd actually walk away with after everything? Because with us, there are zero costs coming out of your pocket. Sometimes the number looks different when you compare apples to apples."

### "I want more money"
**Option A (Explore the Gap):**
> "I respect that. Help me understand — what number would make this work for you?"

**Option B (Reality Check — Gentle):**
> "I wish I could get you more, I really do. The challenge is the data in the area — [restate comps]. But let me take this back to the team and see if there's any creative way to close that gap."

### "Another investor offered me more"
**Option A (Differentiate):**
> "That's great that you have options. Can I ask — is that a cash offer? Because a lot of investors will throw out a high number to lock up a contract, then come back later with inspections and re-negotiations and the number shrinks. With us, what we agree to is what you get. No surprises, no re-trades."

**Option B (Net Comparison):**
> "I appreciate you sharing that. One thing I'd encourage you to look at is the net — are they covering all your closing costs and commissions? Because sometimes a higher number doesn't mean more money in your pocket."

## TRUST / PROCESS OBJECTIONS

### "This sounds too good to be true"
> "I appreciate the honesty, and I understand the skepticism. Here's exactly how it works — we earn our fee from the buyer, not from you. You get every penny we agree to. I can provide references from sellers we've worked with if that would help put your mind at ease."

### "How do you make money?"
> "Great question, and I'm happy to be transparent about it. We earn our fee from the buyer side of the transaction. Your net price is locked in — what we agree to is what you get. The buyer pays our fee on top of that. So our interests are actually aligned — the faster we close, the better for both of us."

### "I don't understand novation / What is this?"
> "It's actually simpler than it sounds. Think of it this way — you get the same experience as a cash offer. You don't fix anything, you don't pay any fees or commissions, and you get your money at closing. The only difference is you end up netting MORE money because we're able to sell to a wider pool of buyers. Everything is in black and white, and I'll walk you through every document so there are no surprises."

### "I need to talk to my lawyer/advisor"
> "Absolutely, I would encourage that. I can send everything over so they can review it, and I'm happy to jump on a call with them to answer any questions directly. What's the best way to get the documents to them?"

## TIMELINE / READINESS OBJECTIONS

### "I need to think about it"
> "I completely understand, and I respect that. Can I ask — what specifically would you like to think over? I want to make sure I've answered all your questions so you have everything you need to make the best decision."

### "I'm not ready to sell yet"
> "No problem at all. When do you think you might be ready? I'd love to stay in touch so when the timing is right, we can move quickly for you."

### "I want to wait and see if the market goes up"
> "I understand that thinking. The thing is, the market data right now is showing [current trend]. And every month you hold, there are carrying costs — mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance. Sometimes waiting actually costs more than it saves. But I respect your decision either way."

---

## DECISION MAKER OBJECTIONS

### "My spouse/partner needs to agree"
> "Of course, that makes total sense. Would it be helpful if I jumped on a quick call with both of you? That way I can answer any questions they have directly and you don't have to play middleman."

### "My kids/family are involved in the decision"
> "I understand — family decisions take time. Would it help if I put together a summary of everything we discussed so you can share it with them? And I'm happy to speak with anyone directly if they have questions."

---

## COMPETITION OBJECTIONS

### "I'm talking to other investors/companies"
> "I think that's smart — you should explore your options. All I'd ask is that you compare apples to apples. Some key things to look at: Are they covering ALL your closing costs? Is their offer truly as-is, or will there be inspections and re-negotiations? And what's your net — the actual amount you walk away with? That's what really matters."

### "I'm thinking about listing with a realtor"
> "That's definitely an option. A few things to consider though — with a realtor, you're typically looking at 6% in commissions, 2-3% in closing costs, and most agents will recommend repairs before listing. Plus the average days on market right now in your area is [X days]. With us, you skip all of that and net [X amount] with no hassle and no waiting. But I understand if you want to explore that route."

### "I got a Zillow/Opendoor offer"
> "Those iBuyer offers can look attractive upfront, but there are usually service fees of 5-7%, plus they do inspections and will deduct repair costs from the offer. By the time you get to closing, the actual net is often a lot less than the initial number. With us, your net price is your net price — no hidden deductions."

## GENERAL RECOVERY

### When you don't have an answer
> "That's a great question, and I want to make sure I give you the right answer. Let me check on that with the team and get back to you. Is there anything else on your mind while I have you?"

### When the seller is getting emotional
> "I hear you, and I can tell this is really important to you. I want you to know — my goal is to find a solution that works for YOU. I'm not here to pressure you into anything. Let's just take it one step at a time."

### When the seller goes silent
> "I know that's a lot to take in. Take your time — is there anything you'd like me to go over again or clarify?"

## DNC / STOP REQUESTS

### "Stop calling me" / "Take me off your list" / "Don't contact me again"
> "I apologize for the inconvenience. I'm removing you from our list right now. You won't hear from us again. Have a good day."

→ IMMEDIATELY flag as DNC in Podio and all systems. No exceptions.
