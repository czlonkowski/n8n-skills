# Seller Outreach Scripts & Templates

Complete scripts for cold calling, SMS, email, and Spanish-language outreach.

---

## 1. Cold Calling Script (TARP Framework)

### Opening (First 10 Seconds — Critical)

```
"Hi, is this [First Name]?

Hey [First Name], my name is [Your Name] with Equity Path Offers.
I'm calling about a property I believe you own at [Address].
Did I catch you at a good time?"
```

### If Yes — Discovery Questions (TARP)

**T — Timeline:**
```
"Are you currently looking to sell, or just exploring options?"
"Is there a specific timeline you're working with?"
"How soon would you ideally like this handled?"
```

**A — Authority:**
```
"Are you the sole owner of the property?"
"Is there anyone else involved in the decision to sell?"
```

**R — Reason/Motivation:**
```
"What's prompting you to consider selling?"
"If you don't mind me asking, what would you do with the money?"
"Have you tried selling before? What happened?"
```

**P — Price:**
```
"Do you have a price in mind?"
"What do you feel the property is worth?"
"Would you be open to a fair cash offer?"
```

### Condition Assessment

```
"How would you describe the condition of the property?"
"Does it need any major repairs — roof, HVAC, foundation?"
"Is anyone currently living in the property?"
"When was the last time you were at the property?"
```

### Close to Appointment

```
"Based on what you've shared, I think we might be able to help.
Here's what I'd like to do — I'd love to take a quick look
at the property and put together a fair cash offer for you.
Would [Day] at [Time] work for a quick visit?"
```

## 2. Objection Handling

### "I'm not interested right now."
```
"Totally understand. Many of the sellers we work with felt the same
way initially. Would it be okay if I followed up in a few weeks?
Things change."
```

### "I need to talk to my spouse/partner/family."
```
"Of course! Would it help if we scheduled a time when both of you
could be on the call?"
```

### "I want full market value."
```
"I completely understand wanting top dollar. What we offer is speed
and certainty — no repairs, no showings, no agent fees. Would it
help to see what a cash offer would look like alongside your other
options?"
```

### "How did you get my number?"
```
"Great question — your property showed up in our research as one
that might be a fit for what we do. We help homeowners sell quickly
for cash. If this isn't a fit, I completely understand and I'll
take you off our list."
```

### "Your offer is too low."
```
"I hear you. That cash number reflects the speed and convenience —
no repairs, no fees, close in 7 days. But I actually have another
option that could get you closer to your number. We can list your
property for retail buyers through our novation program. Would you
like to hear about that?"
```

### "I'm already working with an agent."
```
"That's great — you're in good hands. If for any reason it doesn't
work out or the listing expires, we'd love to be a backup option.
Can I check back in 90 days?"
```

### "Just send me an email."
```
"Absolutely! What's the best email address? And just so I can
tailor the information — is there anything specific you'd want
to see in that email?"
```

## 3. SMS Templates (English)

### Initial Outreach (Requires Prior Consent)
```
Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with Equity Path Offers. We're
interested in making a cash offer on your property at [Address].
Would you be open to a quick conversation? Reply STOP to opt out.
```

### Follow-Up Day 3 (No Response)
```
Hi [Name], just following up on the property at [Address]. We're
still interested in making you a fair cash offer. Any interest?
Reply STOP to opt out.
```

### Post-Voicemail SMS
```
Hi [Name], I just tried calling about your property at [Address].
We buy houses for cash and can close quickly. Give me a call back
when you get a chance! -[Your Name] [Phone]. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

### Re-Engagement (Day 30+)
```
Hi [Name], checking back in on [Address]. Our cash offer still
stands if you're interested. No pressure — just want you to know
the option is there. -[Your Name]. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

### After Qualification (Warm)
```
Hi [Name], great chatting with you about [Address]! I've passed
your info to our acquisitions team. Someone will be reaching out
within 24 hours with exact numbers. -[Your Name], Equity Path Offers
```

### Hot Lead Follow-Up
```
Hi [Name], just wanted to make sure you got my message about
[Address]. We're ready to move forward whenever you are. Call or
text me anytime at [Phone]. -[Your Name]
```

## 7. Outreach Timing Best Practices

| Channel | Best Time | Worst Time |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Cold Call | 10am-12pm, 4pm-6pm local | Before 9am, after 8pm |
| SMS | 10am-2pm local | Before 8am, after 8pm |
| Email | Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am | Weekends, Monday morning |
| Follow-up call | Different time than first attempt | Same time as missed call |

### Key Statistics
- Speed-to-lead under 5 minutes = 21x conversion improvement
- Hybrid approach (call + SMS) = up to 40% higher contact rate
- Cold call to appointment conversion: 2-5% average
- 6-8 touches typically needed before conversion
- 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups, but 44% of salespeople give up after 1
